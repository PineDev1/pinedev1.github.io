Common questions

How do I putt?
Pull back anywhere on the screen and let go. The farther you pull, the harder the shot. Settings > How to Play has animated lessons and a practice green.
I bought Remove Ads but I still see ads.
Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Remove Ads turns off all automatic ads. The optional "watch an ad" offers stay until you switch off Watch-ad offers in Settings.
How do I get my progress on a new iPhone or iPad?
Leave Sync with iCloud on in Settings on both devices and sign in to the same iCloud account. Coins, power-ups, unlocked courses, ball skins and scorecards carry over.
The Daily Challenge says I already played it.
Each daily can be played once. If you leave part-way through, it's saved: tap Continue Daily on the menu to carry on from your last shot. Choosing End Challenge from the pause menu finishes it for the day. A new one arrives every day at midnight your time.
How do I start over?
Settings > Reset All Progress erases coins, power-ups, courses and scorecards on all your devices. Purchases are kept.
